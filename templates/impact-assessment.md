# AI system impact assessment — [system / version / date]

1. **Scope:** task, context, role (provider/deployer), intended and foreseeable uses; why an assessment is needed.
2. **People and participation:** who benefits; who can be harmed; which voices were heard and which are still missing. Do not substitute a technical metric for consultation.
3. **Impact table:** for each affected group state benefit/harm, mechanism, severity, likelihood/uncertainty, evidence, proposed measure, owner and date.
4. **Rights and recourse:** access, nondiscrimination, privacy, autonomy, explanation, correction and appeal where applicable.
5. **Alternatives:** no AI, process redesign, limited pilot, different data or threshold; state relative costs and unknowns.
6. **Related reviews:** separate privacy DPIA, security threat model and applicable legal analysis; cite their owners rather than merging their conclusions.
7. **Decision and dissent:** approve/reject/defer/condition, acceptance owner, outstanding actions, monitoring, stop criteria, review date and dissenting opinion.

Use [ISO/IEC 42005 overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/42005), [NIST AI RMF](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/) and appropriate regulator guidance. This workpaper is an instructional interpretation; it does not reproduce ISO clauses.
