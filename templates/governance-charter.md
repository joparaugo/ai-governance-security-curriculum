# AI governance charter — [organization / version / approval date]

**Scope and exclusions:** organizational units, AI systems, suppliers, use contexts and geography.

**Decision rights:** board sets risk appetite and receives material exceptions; AI governance lead operates inventory and reviews; system owner owns impact and risk; data steward controls data purposes; security lead verifies controls; privacy/legal assess applicable obligations; affected-person representatives can raise and appeal concerns. Identify independent challenge.

**Gates:** intake → classification → impact/privacy/security review → testing → conditional approval → monitoring → change assessment → retirement. For each gate specify owner, required evidence, decision and appeal route.

**Escalation:** high-impact use, untested model change, serious incident, unresolved rights impact or expired exception goes to named governing authority. Record decision, dissent, compensating control, expiration and review date.

**Objectives and metrics:** inventory coverage, time to assess, overdue high-severity risks, contested outcomes, subgroup testing coverage, incident response and remediation time. Review definitions and gaming risk quarterly.

**Review:** at least once per term and after material change. This charter is a course template inspired by [NIST AI RMF Govern](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/) and [ISO/IEC 42001 overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001); it is not a reproduction of standard clauses.
