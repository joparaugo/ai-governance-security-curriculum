Publisher versions and legal amendments are tracked in [resources](resources.md). ISO standards have paid full texts; use the free alternatives in the last column when a library subscription is unavailable.

## Current-version alerts

- EU AI Act rules for high-risk **Annex III** use cases are listed by the Commission as applying from **2 December 2027** and relevant **Annex I** product systems from **2 August 2028** after Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. Other obligations have different dates. Recheck [Commission timeline](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), the [base act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng) and the [amendment](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj/eng) before each term.
- NIST Privacy Framework **1.0** is the released baseline used here; **1.1 was an initial public draft** on the [NIST update page](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/new-projects/privacy-framework-version-11) on 2026-09-25.
- NIST AI RMF 1.0 is in revision; monitor [AI Resource Center](https://airc.nist.gov/). NIST AI 800-2 is identified as an **initial public draft**; AI 800-3 is a research report.
- ISO/IEC 27701:2025 and ISO 19011:2026 replace older commonly cited editions. OWASP LLM Top 10 2026, Agentic Top 10 2026 and AISVS 1.0 are included.
