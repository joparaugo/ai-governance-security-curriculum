## Interpretation rules

1. NIST **AI RMF** describes AI risk outcomes; NIST **SP 800-37 RMF** defines a system security/privacy risk process. ATT&CK addresses enterprise/cloud adversary behavior; ATLAS addresses AI-specific behavior. Neither knowledge base certifies a system.
2. A CAIQ or AI-CAIQ answer is an assertion requiring evidence. A STAR listing, SOC 2 report or ISO/IEC 42001 certificate has a specific subject, time period, criteria and assurance mechanism. None alone measures model fairness or prompt-injection resilience.
3. Require an objective, risk, control, owner, evidence ID, test, exception and review date. Use the [control evidence worksheet](../templates/control-evidence-matrix.csv) and [assurance scope note](../templates/assurance-scope-note.md).
4. COSO, COBIT, AICPA and ISO complete publications may require purchase or institutional access. Course exercises use free publisher overviews and original fictional case materials.
5. MITRE catalogs, CSA questionnaires and STAR submission rules change. Verify the active version before teaching or submitting an assessment.

Extension topics such as [FedRAMP](https://www.fedramp.gov/) for US federal procurement or additional sector laws belong in a course only when the case makes them applicable. Maintain such changes under [MAINTENANCE](../MAINTENANCE.md).
