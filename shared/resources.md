# Publisher and regulator resources

Catalog checked against official publisher pages on 2026-09-25. Live versions, links, legal dates and access conditions can change. These are links, not redistributed standard texts. Required student work relies on free publisher overviews, public guidance, and the original exercises in this repository; paid full texts are optional through an institution. CSA, MITRE, COSO, NIST, ISACA and others have distinct scopes; a mapping never proves compliance or certification.

**95 official sources.** [Coverage audit and assessment map](coverage-audit.md) | [Framework crosswalk](framework-crosswalk.md).

For legal exercises, use the current primary legal text and consult qualified local expertise as needed. A crosswalk is an instructional map, not proof of legal compliance or certification. MITRE ATT&CK and ATLAS are descriptive attack knowledge bases; COSO is governance/internal control guidance; CSA CCM and AICM address cloud and AI controls with separate assurance processes.
