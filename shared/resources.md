# Publisher and regulator resources

Catalog assembled 2026-09-25 from publisher, regulator and standards-body pages; major edition and legal-status claims were checked at that date. Live link availability and edition/status may change. These are *links*, not redistributed standards. Required student reading uses free publisher overviews or public guidance; paid ISO texts are optional through institutional subscriptions.

- **RMF — [AI Risk Management Framework 1.0](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/)** (NIST). voluntary framework; current; revision in progress. Access: free. Govern, Map, Measure, Manage; do not treat the Playbook as a mandatory checklist.
- **PLAY — [AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/)** (NIST). practice guide; living. Access: free. Suggested actions; choose based on context.
- **GAI — [AI RMF Generative AI Profile, AI 600-1](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/ai-risk-management-framework-resources)** (NIST). profile; 2024. Access: free. GenAI-specific risks and actions.
- **CSF — [Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework)** (NIST). voluntary framework; 2.0. Access: free. Govern plus Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
- **SP53 — [SP 800-53 Rev. 5, Security and Privacy Controls](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final)** (NIST). control catalog; Rev. 5; Release 5.2.0 issued 2025; check live dataset. Access: free. Tailor controls; no automatic AI compliance claim.
- **SSDF — [SP 800-218, Secure Software Development Framework](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final)** (NIST). practice framework; final. Access: free. Secure development activities.
- **SSDF_AI — [SP 800-218A, Secure Development Practices for Generative AI and Dual-Use Foundation Models](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/a/final)** (NIST). community profile; final. Access: free. AI-specific SSDF profile.
- **PRIV — [Privacy Framework 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework)** (NIST). voluntary framework; 1.0 current; 1.1 initial public draft. Access: free. Do not label 1.1 final until NIST does.
- **AML — [AI 100-2e2025, Adversarial ML Taxonomy](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ai/100/2/e2025/final)** (NIST). taxonomy; 2025. Access: free. Attacker goals, knowledge, life-cycle stages.
- **EVAL_DRAFT — [AI 800-2, Towards Best Practices for Automated Benchmark Evaluations](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/01/towards-best-practices-automated-benchmark-evaluations)** (NIST). research guidance; initial public draft, 2026. Access: free. Explicitly label as draft in class.
- **EVAL_STATS — [AI 800-3, Expanding the AI Evaluation Toolbox with Statistical Models](https://www.nist.gov/publications/expanding-ai-evaluation-toolbox-statistical-models)** (NIST). research report; 2026. Access: free. Measurement assumptions and uncertainty.
- **MONITOR — [AI 800-4, Challenges to the Monitoring of Deployed AI Systems](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.800-4.pdf)** (NIST). research report; 2026. Access: free. Postdeployment monitoring limitations.
- **ARIA — [Assessing Risks and Impacts of AI (ARIA)](https://ai-challenges.nist.gov/aria)** (NIST). evaluation program; living. Access: free. Model, red-team and field levels.
- **IR — [SP 800-61 Rev. 3, Incident Response Recommendations](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final)** (NIST). guidance; 2025 final. Access: free. Incident response integrated with CSF 2.0.
- **ISO42001 — [ISO/IEC 42001:2023, AI management system](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html)** (ISO/IEC). requirements standard; 2023. Access: paid full text; public overview. Teach original AIMS processes; do not reproduce clauses.
- **ISO23894 — [ISO/IEC 23894:2023, AI risk management](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html)** (ISO/IEC). guidance standard; 2023. Access: paid full text; public overview. AI risks through lifecycle.
- **ISO42005 — [ISO/IEC 42005:2025, AI system impact assessment](https://www.iso.org/standard/42005)** (ISO/IEC). guidance standard; 2025. Access: paid full text; public overview. People and societal impacts.
- **ISO42006 — [ISO/IEC 42006:2025, AIMS certification bodies](https://www.iso.org/standard/42006)** (ISO/IEC). conformity standard; 2025. Access: paid full text; public overview. Distinguish certification body requirements from organizational requirements.
- **ISO38507 — [ISO/IEC 38507:2022, Governance implications of AI](https://www.iso.org/standard/56641.html)** (ISO/IEC). governance guidance; 2022. Access: paid full text; public overview. Governing body oversight.
- **ISO5338 — [ISO/IEC 5338:2023, AI system life cycle processes](https://www.iso.org/standard/81118.html)** (ISO/IEC). process standard; 2023. Access: paid full text; public overview. Lifecycle artifacts and transitions.
- **ISO22989 — [ISO/IEC 22989:2022, AI concepts and terminology](https://www.iso.org/standard/74296.html)** (ISO/IEC). terminology standard; 2022. Access: free per ISO catalog; check availability. Common vocabulary.
- **ISO23053 — [ISO/IEC 23053:2022, ML AI system framework](https://www.iso.org/standard/74438.html)** (ISO/IEC). architecture standard; 2022. Access: paid full text; public overview. System component boundaries.
- **ISO5259 — [ISO/IEC 5259-1:2024, Data quality for analytics and ML](https://www.iso.org/standard/81088.html)** (ISO/IEC). data quality standard; 2024; multi-part series. Access: paid full text; public overview. Data quality scope and measures.
- **ISO25059 — [ISO/IEC 25059:2023, Quality model for AI systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/80655.html)** (ISO/IEC). quality standard; 2023. Access: paid full text; public overview. Specify quality requirements.
- **ISO27001 — [ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Information security management](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001)** (ISO/IEC). requirements standard; 2022. Access: paid full text; public overview. ISMS assurance.
- **ISO27002 — [ISO/IEC 27002:2022, Information security controls](https://www.iso.org/standard/75652.html)** (ISO/IEC). control guidance; 2022. Access: paid full text; public overview. Security control selection.
- **ISO27005 — [ISO/IEC 27005:2022, Information security risk](https://www.iso.org/standard/80585.html)** (ISO/IEC). risk guidance; 2022. Access: paid full text; public overview. Cyber risk methods.
- **ISO27701 — [ISO/IEC 27701:2025, Privacy information management](https://www.iso.org/standard/27701)** (ISO/IEC). requirements and guidance; 2025. Access: paid full text; public overview. PIMS; 2019 edition replaced.
- **ISO31000 — [ISO 31000:2018, Risk management](https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html)** (ISO). guidance standard; 2018; draft revision watch. Access: paid full text; public overview. Risk process independent of domain.
- **ISO19011 — [ISO 19011:2026, Auditing management systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/19011)** (ISO). audit guidance; 2026. Access: paid full text; public overview. Audit evidence and independence.
- **OWASP_LLM — [Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-genai-llm-top-10-2026/)** (OWASP). community risk guide; 2026. Access: free. Awareness taxonomy, not a certification.
- **OWASP_AGENT — [Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/)** (OWASP). community risk guide; 2026. Access: free. Tool permissions, memory and agency.
- **AISVS — [Artificial Intelligence Security Verification Standard 1.0](https://owasp.org/projects/artificial-intelligence-security-verification-standard-aisvs-docs)** (OWASP). testable requirements; 1.0 stable. Access: free. Select verifiable requirements by assurance level.
- **ASVS — [Application Security Verification Standard](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/)** (OWASP). testable requirements; check latest release. Access: free. Conventional application controls.
- **ATLAS — [Adversarial Threat Landscape for AI Systems](https://atlas.mitre.org/)** (MITRE). threat knowledge base; living. Access: free. Map attack paths, not compliance.
- **AICM — [AI Controls Matrix v1.1](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/ai-controls-matrix-v1-1)** (Cloud Security Alliance). control framework; v1.1 June 2026. Access: free registration may apply. Cloud AI control evidence.
- **CISA_AI — [Guidelines for Secure AI System Development](https://www.cisa.gov/ai)** (CISA / UK NCSC). development guidance; living. Access: free. Secure design, development, deployment, operation.
- **OECD — [OECD AI Principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles)** (OECD). intergovernmental principles; updated 2024. Access: free. Public policy and accountability.
- **UNESCO — [Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics)** (UNESCO). intergovernmental recommendation; 2021. Access: free. Human rights and inclusion.
- **COE — [Framework Convention on AI and Human Rights](https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence/the-framework-convention-on-artificial-intelligence)** (Council of Europe). treaty; 2024; ratification varies. Access: free. Verify ratification by jurisdiction.
- **IMDA — [Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI](https://www.imda.gov.sg/AI)** (IMDA / AI Verify Foundation). voluntary guidance; 2024; new agentic work watch. Access: free. Asia-Pacific comparison.
- **EU_ACT — [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng)** (EU). law; amended in 2026. Access: free. Read with 2026/1744 and Commission timeline.
- **EU_AMEND — [Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, AI Omnibus](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj/eng)** (EU). law amendment; 2026. Access: free. Changes high-risk implementation dates.
- **EU_TIMELINE — [AI Act application timeline](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)** (European Commission). official guidance; checked 2026-09-25. Access: free. Recheck before every teaching term.
- **GDPR — [Regulation (EU) 2016/679, GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng)** (EU). law; check consolidated text. Access: free. Personal data duties differ from AI Act.
- **DPIA — [Data Protection Impact Assessment guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/topics/accountability-and-compliance-tools/data-protection-impact-assessment_en)** (EDPB). regulator guidance; living. Access: free. Privacy-specific impact assessment.
- **ICO — [AI and data protection risk toolkit](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/ai-and-data-protection-risk-toolkit/)** (UK ICO). regulator tool; living. Access: free. Practical worksheet; UK law context.
- **FERPA — [Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act resources](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/topic/family-educational-rights-privacy-act-ferpa)** (US Department of Education). sector law resources; living. Access: free. Check applicability to scenario institution.
- **ISO5259_2 — [ISO/IEC 5259-2:2024, Data quality model and measures](https://www.iso.org/standard/81860.html)** (ISO/IEC). data quality standard; 2024. Access: paid full text; public overview. Specify and report dataset quality measures.
- **ISO5259_3 — [ISO/IEC 5259-3:2024, Data quality management](https://www.iso.org/standard/81092.html)** (ISO/IEC). data quality standard; 2024. Access: paid full text; public overview. Manage quality of data used for ML.
- **ISO5259_4 — [ISO/IEC 5259-4:2024, Data quality process framework](https://www.iso.org/standard/81093.html)** (ISO/IEC). data quality standard; 2024. Access: paid full text; public overview. Lifecycle and labeling processes.
- **ISO5259_5 — [ISO/IEC 5259-5:2025, Data quality governance](https://www.iso.org/standard/84150.html)** (ISO/IEC). data quality governance; 2025. Access: paid full text; public overview. Governing-body accountability for data quality.
- **ISO24027 — [ISO/IEC TR 24027:2021, Bias in AI](https://www.iso.org/standard/77607.html)** (ISO/IEC). technical report; 2021. Access: paid full text; public overview. Bias sources across data, model and use.
- **ISO12791 — [ISO/IEC TS 12791:2024, Treatment of unwanted bias](https://www.iso.org/standard/84110.html)** (ISO/IEC). technical specification; 2024. Access: paid full text; public overview. Classification and regression bias treatments.
- **ISO24028 — [ISO/IEC TR 24028:2020, Trustworthiness overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/77608.html)** (ISO/IEC). technical report; 2020. Access: paid full text; public overview. Trustworthiness factors and limits.
- **ISO24029 — [ISO/IEC TR 24029-1:2021, Neural-network robustness overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/77609.html)** (ISO/IEC). technical report; 2021. Access: paid full text; public overview. Robustness assessment.
- **ISO24029_2 — [ISO/IEC 24029-2:2023, Formal robustness methods](https://www.iso.org/standard/79804.html)** (ISO/IEC). method standard; 2023. Access: paid full text; public overview. Formal method scope and assumptions.
- **ISO29119 — [ISO/IEC TR 29119-11:2020, Testing AI-based systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/79016.html)** (ISO/IEC). testing guidance; 2020. Access: paid full text; public overview. Test oracle and AI testing challenges.
- **ISO42119 — [ISO/IEC TS 42119-2:2025, Testing AI systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/84127.html)** (ISO/IEC). technical specification; 2025. Access: paid full text; public overview. Risk-based AI testing process.
- **IEEE7000 — [IEEE 7000-2021, Ethical concerns during system design](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/7000/6781/)** (IEEE SA). process standard; 2021. Access: check IEEE GET/institutional access. Values elicitation and traceability.
- **IEEE7001 — [IEEE 7001-2021, Transparency of autonomous systems](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/7001/6929/)** (IEEE SA). transparency standard; 2021. Access: check institutional access. Measurable transparency for stakeholders.
- **IEEE7003 — [IEEE 7003-2024, Algorithmic bias considerations](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/7003/11357/)** (IEEE SA). bias standard; 2024. Access: check institutional access. Bias quality control and use boundaries.

For law assignments, use the current primary legal text and consult local counsel where applicable. A framework crosswalk is an instructional mapping, not proof of legal compliance or certification.
