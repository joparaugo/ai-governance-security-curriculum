# Transfer cases for new assessments

These short cases change the institutional context without introducing real persons or data. Use them after the CampusAssist case to test whether learners can transfer a method. No case makes a legal classification for the student; verify jurisdiction and statutory facts with primary sources.

## A. Clinic scheduling assistant

A community clinic deploys a vendor chatbot to translate appointment requests and recommend a triage queue. It stores prompts for 18 months, may route messages to an external model and gives nurses 30 seconds to review urgent flags. Its validation group under-represents speakers of one language. **Student challenge:** map privacy and safety impacts; design language-specific error measures, secure retention, meaningful nurse override and incident escalation. **Applicable modules:** 1:07–09, 2:04–08, 3:06–11, 4:04–05. Start with [NIST RMF](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/) and the [Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework); ask local counsel about health-sector rules.

## B. Small-business lending model

A lender buys a scoring API. The vendor reports a high area-under-curve measure but provides no false-decline rates or counterfactual study. Staff can override a decline but do not see reasons. **Student challenge:** define the decision boundary, group harm metrics, human oversight, procurement rights and scope of legal analysis. **Applicable modules:** 1:03–08, 2:02–06, 4:04–08. Compare [OECD principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles) and [EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng) only after defining location and use.

## C. Public benefits information agent

A city uses an AI agent to summarize public eligibility rules and prepare, but not submit, an application. A retrieved page contains instructions to change the applicant's address. The agent tool has broad write permission. **Student challenge:** distinguish misinformation, prompt injection and unauthorized writes; design least privilege, approval, records correction and a public complaint route. **Applicable modules:** 2:08–11, 3:05–07, 4:09–11. Use [OWASP Agentic Top 10](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/) and [AISVS](https://owasp.org/projects/artificial-intelligence-security-verification-standard-aisvs-docs).

## D. Frontier model evaluation partnership

A university lab is asked to evaluate a capable third-party model with restricted API access. The partner requests publication of a single aggregate safety score but the researchers worry about hidden test contamination, dual-use detail and time-limited access. **Student challenge:** pre-register measurement targets and limitations, test authorized capabilities, protect sensitive findings, and specify independent review and disclosure criteria. **Applicable modules:** 1:04–07, 2:01–02 and 2:08–12, 3:02 and 3:09, 4:11. Use [NIST AI 800-2 draft](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/01/towards-best-practices-automated-benchmark-evaluations), [AI 800-3](https://www.nist.gov/publications/expanding-ai-evaluation-toolbox-statistical-models) and [ARIA](https://ai-challenges.nist.gov/aria). Do not claim the single score measures all risks.

**Instructor substitution:** replace names, change one key fact, supply an evidence packet modeled on E-01–E-08, and score with the same rubric. A lawful-use assessment is a student question, not an answer asserted by this brief.
