# Lab 4: internal audit and incident tabletop

**Time:** 90 minutes. **Use:** Course 4 weeks 7–9. **Source anchors:** [ISO 19011:2026 overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/19011), [NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final), [NIST AI RMF](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/).

## Part A — 35-minute audit

Use evidence E-01–E-08 in the [case packet](../shared/case-campusassist.md). In groups assign auditor, system owner, student representative and observer. State audit objective, scope, criteria, sample and conflicts of interest. Trace one decision from approval to test evidence. Draft two findings using `criterion / evidence / condition / effect / recommendation`; classify as finding or unanswered request, and ask what corroborates the record. Use the [audit workpaper](../templates/audit-workpaper.md).

## Part B — 40-minute incident

**00:00 inject:** 14 blocked simulated email attempts triggered by a public page. **00:10:** vendor model version changed two days earlier. **00:20:** a student says a private advisor note appeared in their chatbot answer; the first sample is a screenshot without source log. **00:30:** campus media requests a statement. Teams must choose incident commander, preserve logs and versions, isolate risky tools, verify any successful outbound action, consult privacy/legal teams about notification, provide affected-student support and set restart criteria. Record decisions and uncertainty.

## Debrief — 15 minutes

Compare how teams prevented evidence destruction and handled uncertain allegations. A defensible answer distinguishes blocked attempts from proven disclosure, does not dismiss either, and gives a timed investigation plan. Submit one finding, one incident timeline and one board update. No real service is accessed.
