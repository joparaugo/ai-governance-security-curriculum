# Fairness and subgroup evaluation
### AIE 420 · Week 05

<!-- Speaker: Ask for one case assumption before revealing the outline. -->

## Today’s decision

Evening students experience a higher false negative rate in the synthetic sample.

**What should the institution do next, and what evidence would change your mind?**

<!-- Speaker: Give groups 90 seconds to make a first call. -->
---
## 1 · System and context

- Compare relevant group error rates and discuss why parity criteria can conflict.
- Identify an affected person and the decision owner.

<!-- Speaker: Ask for the boundary of the claim. -->
---
## 2 · Competing interpretations

- Report group denominators, confidence/uncertainty and possible label bias.
- Record a plausible alternative explanation.

<!-- Speaker: Challenge a confident generalization. -->
---
## 3 · Operational test

- Use outcomes and harms to select a fairness measure with stakeholder input.
- Specify observable evidence and review date.

<!-- Speaker: Ask who verifies this claim. -->

Calculate group-specific rates; draft a cautious decision memo.

**Deliver:** Fairness audit with metric choice and caveats
