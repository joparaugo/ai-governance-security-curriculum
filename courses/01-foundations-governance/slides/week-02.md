# Values, rights and tradeoffs
### GOV 310 · Week 02

<!-- Speaker: Ask for one case assumption before revealing the outline. -->
---
## Today’s decision

A student misses aid outreach because a contact field was stale.

**What should the institution do next, and what evidence would change your mind?**

<!-- Speaker: Give groups 90 seconds to make a first call. -->
---
## 1 · System and context

- Translate broad principles such as fairness and accountability into observable behavior and decision owners.
- Identify an affected person and the decision owner.

<!-- Speaker: Ask for the boundary of the claim. -->
---
## 2 · Competing interpretations

- Separate individual harms from collective impacts and opportunity costs; ask who can contest a decision.
- Record a plausible alternative explanation.

<!-- Speaker: Challenge a confident generalization. -->
---
## 3 · Operational test

- Explicitly state the ethical lens and tradeoff rather than calling a system “ethical” in general.
- Specify observable evidence and review date.

<!-- Speaker: Ask who verifies this claim. -->
---
## Studio: produce an artifact

Run a stakeholder hearing with students, faculty and registrar roles.

**Deliver:** Stakeholder/impact table with two conflicting values and resolution path

## Challenge and exit

- What is still uncertain?
- Which person or team is accountable?
- Which evidence would justify a different decision?
