## Today’s decision

The counseling office adds automated messages using an unregistered chatbot.

**What should the institution do next, and what evidence would change your mind?**

## 1 · System and context

- Build an inventory that includes shadow tools, use purpose, data sensitivity, supplier and integration.
- Identify an affected person and the decision owner.

<!-- Speaker: Ask for the boundary of the claim. -->
---
## 2 · Competing interpretations

- Classify use cases by context and consequence rather than by model size alone.
- Record a plausible alternative explanation.

<!-- Speaker: Challenge a confident generalization. -->
---
## 3 · Operational test

- Record assumptions and a review date because risk class can change after repurposing.
- Specify observable evidence and review date.
