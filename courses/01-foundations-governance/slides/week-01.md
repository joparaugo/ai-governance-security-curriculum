# What counts as an AI system?
### GOV 310 · Week 01

<!-- Speaker: Ask for one case assumption before revealing the outline. -->
---
## Today’s decision

CampusAssist flags students for outreach; an advisor, not the score alone, chooses contact.

**What should the institution do next, and what evidence would change your mind?**

<!-- Speaker: Give groups 90 seconds to make a first call. -->
---
## 1 · System and context

- Distinguish a model from a complete sociotechnical system: data, interfaces, people, rules and appeals all affect outcomes.
- Identify an affected person and the decision owner.

<!-- Speaker: Ask for the boundary of the claim. -->
---
## 2 · Competing interpretations

- Describe intended use, foreseeable misuse and excluded uses before judging trustworthiness.
- Record a plausible alternative explanation.

<!-- Speaker: Challenge a confident generalization. -->
---
## 3 · Operational test

- Draw the boundary at the actual decision workflow, including the advisor who can override an alert.
- Specify observable evidence and review date.

<!-- Speaker: Ask who verifies this claim. -->
---

Teams draw a boundary diagram with upstream data and downstream actions.

**Deliver:** One-page system card with actors and intended/excluded uses

## Challenge and exit

- What is still uncertain?
- Which person or team is accountable?
- Which evidence would justify a different decision?
