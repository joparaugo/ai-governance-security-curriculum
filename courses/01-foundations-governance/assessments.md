# Midcourse assessment bank and public key

Use these as formative items or change facts for summative use. Suggested time: 60 minutes; eight multiple-choice items (5 points each) and two short response items (30 points each). Allow open publisher sources but require justification and denominators.

## Student questions

**1.** Which item belongs outside the *model* but inside the deployed *AI system*?

- A. Weights
- B. Feature vector
- C. Advisor appeal procedure
- D. Training optimizer

**2.** The vendor says “NIST certified.” What is the first response?

- A. Accept it as law
- B. Ask which certification scheme and scope it means
- C. Skip the AI inventory
- D. Assume ISO 42001 covers it

**3.** A risk register row should identify:

- A. Only a score
- B. Cause, event, harm, owner and treatment
- C. Only the vendor
- D. Only an ISO number

**4.** What best supports meaningful human oversight?

- A. A checkbox marked human
- B. A reviewer with time, context and override authority
- C. A monthly slide deck
- D. A model size limit

**5.** Which is the best use of a standards crosswalk?

- A. Automatic certification
- B. A traceable map of related outcomes and evidence
- C. An exemption from law
- D. A substitute for source text

**6.** Which statement about a DPIA is most accurate?

- A. It replaces every AI impact assessment
- B. It specifically assesses data-protection risks where applicable
- C. It is a model benchmark
- D. It requires all AI to stop

**7.** A staff member starts using an unregistered chatbot. What first?

- A. Delete all inventories
- B. Record use and assess purpose, data and access
- C. Ignore low-cost tools
- D. Certify the model

**8.** Which board decision is best documented?

- A. Approve forever
- B. Pilot with owners, tests, appeal and stop triggers
- C. Outsource all risk
- D. Rely on 92% accuracy

**9. Short response (30 points).** Explain why the 92% accuracy claim in the case cannot decide deployment. Show reasoning and one source or case reference.

**10. Short response (30 points).** Draft a two-sentence pilot condition that a board could later verify. Show reasoning and one source or case reference.

## Instructor key and scoring notes

- 1: **C**. The appeal procedure is part of the decision workflow.

- 2: **B**. The claim needs a concrete scheme and evidence; AI RMF is voluntary.

- 3: **B**. A scenario and accountable treatment make the register actionable.

- 4: **B**. Oversight must be operable in the actual workflow.

- 5: **B**. A crosswalk is a teaching/assurance map, not an equivalence declaration.

- 6: **B**. Privacy and broader societal impact assessments address different questions.

- 7: **B**. Inventory and triage should include shadow use.

- 8: **B**. A conditional decision matches uncertain evidence and defines follow-up.

- 9: Look for cohort and prevalence, error costs, group rates, labels, uncertainty and intervention capacity. Award 10 points for method, 10 for correct application, 5 for uncertainty and 5 for a cited anchor or case evidence.

- 10: Name an owner, test population, threshold or observable evidence, review date and stop action. Award 10 points for method, 10 for correct application, 5 for uncertainty and 5 for a cited anchor or case evidence.

**Item analysis:** after class, record which distractors attracted learners and revise ambiguous wording. Never infer competence from these ten items alone; use applied artifacts and defense.

## Supplementary source-application check (formative)

**Prompt:** E-01 authorizes only a pilot. The board wants the internal auditor to set the email-tool approval rule and later audit its effectiveness. Name two distinct roles using [IIA Three Lines](https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/documents/) and one [COSO board](https://www.coso.org/corporate-governance-guiding-principles) question. **Key:** management owns design and operation, risk/compliance challenges it, internal audit independently reviews it; the board asks for stop criteria, unresolved risk and an independent escalation route. Award credit for a conflict safeguard if audit previously designed the rule.
