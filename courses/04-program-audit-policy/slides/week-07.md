# Audit program and independence
### GRC 510 · Week 07

<!-- Speaker: Ask for one case assumption before revealing the outline. -->
---
## Today’s decision

The same program manager approved and self-audited the pilot.

**What should the institution do next, and what evidence would change your mind?**

<!-- Speaker: Give groups 90 seconds to make a first call. -->
---
## 1 · System and context

- Set audit criteria, scope, sampling approach, competence and independence.
- Identify an affected person and the decision owner.

<!-- Speaker: Ask for the boundary of the claim. -->
---
## 2 · Competing interpretations

- Request original evidence and trace one decision from approval through monitoring.
- Record a plausible alternative explanation.

<!-- Speaker: Challenge a confident generalization. -->
---
## 3 · Operational test

- Write findings with requirement, condition, cause, effect and recommendation.
- Specify observable evidence and review date.

<!-- Speaker: Ask who verifies this claim. -->
---
## Source comparison and evidence

- Assign first-line model approvals, second-line compliance challenge and independent internal audit. Form an audit objective, criteria and sample, and plan safeguards if an auditor helped design a control.
- **Deliver:** Independent audit plan with conflicts and safeguards.

<!-- Speaker: Insist on claim boundaries and a testable evidence request. -->
---
## Studio: produce an artifact

Audit a synthetic evidence pack and interview the system owner.

**Deliver:** Audit plan and two supported findings

## Challenge and exit

- What is still uncertain?
- Which person or team is accountable?
- Which evidence would justify a different decision?
