# Midcourse assessment bank and public key

Use these as formative items or change facts for summative use. Suggested time: 60 minutes; eight multiple-choice items (5 points each) and two short response items (30 points each). Allow open publisher sources but require justification and denominators.

## Student questions

**1.** ISO/IEC 42001 is primarily a standard for:

- A. One model’s weights
- B. An organizational AI management system
- C. EU legal certification
- D. Prompt syntax

**2.** An internal auditor of the pilot should ideally:

- A. Audit own approval
- B. Have suitable competence and independence
- C. Accept brochures as evidence
- D. Avoid sampling

**3.** A supplier assurance claim should specify:

- A. Only a badge
- B. Scope, criteria, evidence, date and limitations
- C. A slogan
- D. Only a model size

**4.** Which contract term addresses model changes?

- A. Unlimited unilateral updates
- B. Advance notice, evaluation and rollback rights
- C. No retention limits
- D. No audit access

**5.** The EU AI Act high-risk Annex III date as checked 2026-09-25 is:

- A. All duties expired 2024
- B. 2 December 2027, subject to recheck
- C. No EU law exists
- D. Always 2 August 2026

**6.** The Council of Europe convention applies in a country based on:

- A. An OECD dashboard
- B. Relevant signature/ratification and national legal status
- C. A blog post
- D. Any AI purchase

**7.** A useful management review metric is:

- A. Only staff training completion
- B. Overdue high-risk remediation with owner and age
- C. Number of badges
- D. Only model parameters

**8.** An audit finding requires:

- A. Unverified rumor
- B. Criteria, evidence, condition and consequence
- C. A logo
- D. The auditor’s preference

**9. Short response (30 points).** Draft a finding based on E-04 in the case. Show reasoning and one source or case reference.

**10. Short response (30 points).** Name two facts required before classifying the EU partner use. Show reasoning and one source or case reference.

## Instructor key and scoring notes

- 1: **B**. It sets AIMS requirements.

- 2: **B**. Independence and evidence strengthen assurance.

- 3: **B**. Bounded claims are reviewable.

- 4: **B**. Change control permits reassessment.

- 5: **B**. The 2026 amendment extended that category; verify current text each term.

- 6: **B**. Treaty status is jurisdiction-specific.

- 7: **B**. Decision-oriented indicators reveal actionable program gaps.

- 8: **B**. Findings should be traceable and proportionate.

- 9: Criteria: approved evaluation protocol; condition: aggregate figures without group denominators/version; effect: limited assurance; recommend rerun with source evidence. Award 10 points for method, 10 for correct application, 5 for uncertainty and 5 for a cited anchor or case evidence.

- 10: Accept intended purpose, provider/deployer roles, whether access to education is determined, exemptions, location, versions and current law. Award 10 points for method, 10 for correct application, 5 for uncertainty and 5 for a cited anchor or case evidence.

**Item analysis:** after class, record which distractors attracted learners and revise ambiguous wording. Never infer competence from these ten items alone; use applied artifacts and defense.

## Supplementary source-application check (formative)

**Prompt:** E-09 to E-11 show CAIQ and AI-CAIQ answers plus unverified STAR/SOC 2 marketing claims. Name one [cloud CAIQ](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/star-level-1-security-questionnaire-caiq-v4-1) question, one [AI-CAIQ](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/ai-controls-matrix-v1-1) question and one independent assurance fact to verify. **Key:** cloud IAM/log evidence versus model-change/AI control evidence; obtain actual period, system scope, criteria, status and exceptions for any STAR, STAR for AI or SOC 2 claim. A filled questionnaire alone is a self-assertion.
