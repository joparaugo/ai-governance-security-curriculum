# GRC 510: Enterprise AI Governance, Audit, and Public Policy

Build an operating AI governance program, audit evidence, manage suppliers and incidents, and compare policy regimes.

[Teach this course](syllabus.md) · [Instructor guide](instructor-guide.md) · [Assignments](assignments.md) · [Assessments](assessments.md) · [Common case](../../shared/case-campusassist.md) · [Resource catalog](../../shared/resources.md)

## Learning outcomes

1. Design an AI management system with roles, objectives, controls and management review.
2. Assess supplier obligations, contractual controls and shared responsibility.
3. Plan and perform an evidence-based internal audit.
4. Compare legal and international policy instruments by authority and scope.
5. Present a board-level roadmap, incident exercise and integrated capstone decision.
